[![Build](https://github.com/dkavolis/Ferram-Aerospace-Research/actions/workflows/package.yml/badge.svg?branch=master)](https://github.com/dkavolis/Ferram-Aerospace-Research/actions/workflows/package.yml)

Fork of FARc meant for more of a focus on realism.
Thank you to dkavolis for creating the original mod.
Developed with the assistance of Google Antigravity and Github Copilot.
***Every non-human change was thoroughly checked and playtested by a human***
Meant for KSP 1.12.5, will probably work on 1.12.x

### Major additions:
- Polhamus LERX Vortex lift
- Downstream stall delay
- Winglet vortex diffusion
- Realistic induced drag reduction near terrain and runways due to the Mirror-vortex ground effect
- Aeroelastic hinge moment and control surface blowback
- Hypersonic waverider compression lift

  All of these things have an autodetect feature but can be manual overrided in the Part Action Window.
